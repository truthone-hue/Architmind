Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This is an English translation provided for convenience. The French version is the reference text: if the two differ, the French version applies.

This policy explains what data Architmind CI uses, why, who it is shared with and how to exercise your rights.

Data controller

Hunter Agency, publisher of Architmind CI. Contact: Architmind.ci@gmail.com.

Data used

  • Account: name, e-mail address, profile (architect, private individual…), main city. The password is handled by Firebase Authentication: it is never visible, neither to us nor to other users.
  • Projects: for each plan you save, its name and settings: plot area and shape, city, street side, type of house, number of bedrooms, level of finish, cost per m², share of the plot covered, options, the text of the "Your wishes" field, edited rooms and 3D model settings. A plan that is generated but not saved is not kept.
  • Usage: the number of plans generated during the day, to apply your plan's limit.
  • Subscription: the chosen plan, its dates, the amount, the payment method used (Mobile Money or card) and the payment reference.
  • Notifications: payment, renewal and expiry messages.

Your card numbers and Mobile Money codes are entered only on Paystack's page. They are never passed on to us.

When you sign up, we check that the part of your address after the "@" (for example "gmail.com") can receive e-mails. Only this domain name is sent to Google's DNS service, never the full address. A confirmation e-mail is then sent to you by Firebase.

We do not collect your location, your contacts or your photos.

We do not use any audience measurement tool.

What others can see

Nothing. Architmind CI has no public profile, no messaging and no sharing between accounts: your name, e-mail address, projects and payments are visible only to you, once signed in. Only the Architmind CI team can access them, to run the service or answer a request from you.

Where your data is stored

  • Your account, projects, subscription and notifications are saved in the Firebase database (Cloud Firestore), on Google's servers.
  • Your password is kept by Firebase Authentication in an unreadable form.
  • Your payment details are kept by Paystack; we only keep the reference, the amount, the plan and the date.
  • On your device: a copy of your projects and of the app, for instant display and offline use.

Why

  • To provide the service: create, save and find your plans on your devices.
  • To manage your account and subscription, and send receipts and expiry reminders.
  • To tell you, in the site's notifications, about new features.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent fraud.

This processing is necessary to perform the terms of use and to meet our accounting obligations. We do no advertising, reselling or profiling of your data.

Retention period

Your data is kept as long as your account exists. If you ask for it to be deleted, it is erased within 30 days, except the payment information that the law requires us to keep for accounting purposes.

Deleting your data

  • A project: open "My plans", then "Delete" and "Confirm". It is erased from your account immediately.
  • Your account and all your data: write to Architmind.ci@gmail.com from the account's e-mail address. We delete the account, projects and notifications within 30 days and confirm it to you.
  • Data kept on your device: sign out, then clear the site's data in your browser settings, or uninstall the app.

Security

Exchanges are encrypted (HTTPS). The database security rules prevent one account from reading another's data. The subscription and payments can only be changed by our server, after checking with Paystack.

On your device

The app keeps on your device the last e-mail address used, to pre-fill the sign-in form, your language choice, and a copy of your data and of the app, to work offline. There are no advertising cookies and no audience measurement tools. A banner reminds you of this on your first visit.

Your rights

In accordance with Ivorian law no. 2013-450 of 19 June 2013 on the protection of personal data, you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, and object to its use. Write to Architmind.ci@gmail.com. You can also contact the Telecommunications/ICT Regulatory Authority of Côte d'Ivoire (ARTCI).
