Refund policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This is an English translation provided for convenience. The French version is the reference text: if the two differ, the French version applies.

The Medium and Max plans are paid monthly, with no automatic renewal: nothing is charged without your action.

The rule

A payment is refunded only in the event of a major malfunction on our side, reported within 7 days of payment. In all other cases, there is no refund.

What counts as a major malfunction

  • Plan not activated: the payment was charged but the paid plan was not activated on your account, and we did not fix the problem within 3 working days of your report.
  • Double charge: the same plan was charged twice for the same period. The extra payment is refunded.
  • Service unusable: an essential feature of your plan (plan generation, 3D model or plan downloads) could not be used for more than 72 hours in a row because of an outage on our side.

Conditions to be met

All of these conditions must be met:

  • The request is sent within 7 days of payment.
  • The problem comes from Architmind CI, not from your phone, your browser or your internet connection.
  • You reported the problem to us and gave us 3 working days to fix it, without result.
  • You include the payment reference and, if possible, a screenshot of the problem.
  • Your account has not been used abusively or contrary to the terms of use.

What is not refunded

  • A change of mind, or a plan paid for but little or not used.
  • A plan, style or estimate you do not like: the plans are indicative sketches.
  • Rejection of the project by an architect, a town hall or an administration.
  • A mistake in choosing the plan (Medium instead of Max, for example).
  • Slowness or a temporary outage of less than 72 hours.
  • Any request sent more than 7 days after payment.

When there is no refund, your plan remains active until its end date, then your account returns to the free Basic plan, without losing your projects.

Making a request

  1. Write to Architmind.ci@gmail.com with your account's e-mail address.
  2. Give the payment reference, shown on the receipt you received by e-mail, and the reason for the request.
  3. We reply within 3 working days. We may ask you for details to check the problem.

An accepted refund is made by Paystack, to the payment method used (Mobile Money or card). It usually arrives within 5 to 10 working days, depending on the bank or the operator.
